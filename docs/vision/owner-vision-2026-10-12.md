## Decisions the owner gave in the same conversation (2026-10-12)

- Decision 0021: madarch is a standalone engine and CLI; the skill set (shady2k-skills) owns the skills, forms, templates and content checks; the boundary is a published versioned contract of the product repository format. No repository depends on another (the skill set keeps a copy of the versioned schema; it never checks out madarch).
- The product manifest (the kinds registry, `contract: 1`) declares the structure: stages; entity types (name, id prefix, folder, stage, hint, allowed statuses); per type its link types (field, target types, backlink label, cardinality, REQUIRED). Required links belong in the manifest. The engine draws links, backlinks and structural findings generically and picks manifest changes up live (example: a product with UC and no US adds US to its manifest, and madarch picks it up at once, with its links).
- The skill set defines the docs/ structure for all its entities and ships document templates the product may override.
- One source: the product repository/wiki holds everything (security requirements loaded into it; Storybook-like components and designs built into the wiki; designers move from Figma into the wiki). Each type declares its storage form (document, task, model element, component).
- One graph, product-scoped global ids, no duplicates ever; federation with links to other products.
- To-be covers additions, changes and removals (decommissioning is kept as history), tied to milestones.
- No saved graph slices: the graph is computed when needed.
- Approvals bind to roles, not persons (a person may be away); simple e-signature.
- Actions/executors and the main chat: Lifemodel (the owner's project), joined by a published contract.
- Contract v1 reserves now: storage form per type, product-scoped ids, lifecycle with milestones, roles section; the v1 engine implements documents only.
