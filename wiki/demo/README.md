# Fieldnote

A small home for working notes: the questions we carry around, the
half-formed ideas we keep returning to, and the small decisions that
turn a note into something usable. Fieldnote is built for a single
person first; sharing is a later concern.

What you will find here:

- How we think about reading and writing notes while moving between tasks
- The reading experience and why it comes before every other feature
- Research on typography and long-session comfort
- The decisions we have already made, and the ones still open

Start with [From idea to product](docs/vision.md). If you want one
worked example of how we decide things, read
[Owning the reader](docs/decisions/0001-own-the-reader.md).
