# Q-001 — What does the reader want first: the place they left, or the top?

Concerns: UC-001, VISION
Who can answer: the reader of the notes home, asked after two weeks of use

## Question
When a reader returns to a long note, should the view open where they left
it last time, or always at the top of the note?

## What settles it
The reader's own answer after two weeks with the home, against the rule
that nothing moves while a page settles.
