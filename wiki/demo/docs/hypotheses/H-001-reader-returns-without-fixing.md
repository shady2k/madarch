# H-001 — A reader rereads when the return costs nothing

Grew from: VISION, S-001

## Belief
We believe that a reader rereads old notes when opening one shows it
readable in place, with nothing to fix first.

## Test
One reader uses the notes home for two weeks and names every note they
returned to; the reading view measures the time from open to settled.

## Metric
The share of returned notes that were read without an edit made first.

## Threshold
At least four of five returns with no edit before the read.
