## Question and assumptions
The accepted measure holds a reading column of 60 to 75 characters. Wide
diagrams and tables do not fit it; the assumption so far is that they
scroll inside their own containers.

## Perspectives and possibilities
A wider second measure for wide blocks; a fold; a zoom. Each costs
calmness somewhere else.
